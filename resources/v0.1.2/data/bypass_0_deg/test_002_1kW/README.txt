raw folder contains the raw, unedited csv's of pressure, temperature, power, and flow data collected during testing. These data sets are then processed through "extract_steady_state.py" 

processed folder contains the csv's of steady state data from the raw tests, typically in 10 minute intervals. f

Tests completed on 11/13/26
Bypass valve was held at 0 deg fully open, and the inline valve was swept from 0-60 deg in 15 deg intervals. 
Power was held constant at 1kW, and the heat exchanger flow was held constant at 1 gpm
